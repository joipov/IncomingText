# Incoming Text

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Damage, healing, and misses that land on you, in one list.** The list sits on the left. Damage uses the spell school color. A critical hit uses its own size and color.

**Show damage**, **Show healing**, and **Show misses** choose which lines appear.

**Font**, **Font size**, **Crit size**, **Opacity**, **Lines shown**, **Text align**, and **Grow** sit on one settings page with the colors for physical hits, critical hits, spell schools, healing, and misses. Fonts from other addons appear when the shared media library is installed. Turn **Lock position** off to drag the corner square, then right-click it to lock. The minimap button is on and opens settings.

`/it` opens settings. So does the addon compartment. `/it test` shows sample lines.
