# Incoming Text

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Damage, healing, status, and power, each in its own list.** Misses stay on the damage list. `+Combat` and `-Combat` stay on the status list. The mana, rage, energy, or focus a completed cast spends stays on the power list. Each list has its own place and look.

**Damage**, **Healing**, **Status**, and **Power** are separate settings pages. Each has enable, lock, font, size, opacity, fade time, lines, align, grow, animate entry, and its own colors. Damage, healing, and status also have crit size. **Text align** is left, center, or right. **Animate entry** fades a new line in and slides it into place. The damage page has **Show misses**. The status page has **Show combat**, which prints `+Combat` on entering combat and `-Combat` on leaving. The power page prints that cast's cost, such as `-8%` or `-15`. Mana is a percent of your maximum by default. Rage, energy, and focus show the cost itself, and each resource has its own percent setting. Mana is blue, rage is red, energy is yellow, and focus is orange. A normal heal is green, and a critical heal is a lighter green. An amount the client hides is still shown. The power list starts to the right of center. The main page has the same **Lock position**, font, size, opacity, fade time, lines, align, grow, and animate entry rows. Changing one of those sets the same row on all four lists, and those rows move to the new value. Crit size on the main page sets damage, healing, and status. Fonts from other addons appear when the shared media library is installed. Turn **Lock position** off to drag that list's area or an edge. Sample lines show where the text will sit until a real line is on screen. Right-click it to lock. The minimap button is on by default and opens settings.

`/it` opens settings. So does the addon compartment. `/it test` shows sample lines.
