# Combat Text

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Your hits and the hits you take, in two lists.** Incoming sits on the left. Outgoing sits on the right. Damage uses the spell school color. A critical hit uses its own size and color.

Incoming lines are damage you take and healing that lands on you. Outgoing lines are damage and healing on your target. In a group, outgoing lines include other players, because the game does not say who landed the hit. **Line order** places the icon, the number, and the spell name. **Shorten spell names** uses initials. **Icons on normal hits** and **Icons on crits** add the spell icon. **Icons on white hits** adds the weapon from your latest swing. Damage over time stays as a number. A buff does not name the next number. Damage keeps a name only from a spell that can hit an enemy, and a heal only from a helpful spell.

A cast that dies on your target shows as an interrupted line. The line does not name your kick. The spell on that line follows the same order.

Settings are split into **General**, **Incoming**, **Outgoing**, and **Colors**. **Show incoming** and **Show outgoing** hide a list on their own. Each list has its own **Font**, **Font size**, **Crit size**, **Opacity**, **Lines shown**, **Text align**, and **Grow**. Fonts from other addons appear when the shared media library is installed. **Colors** covers physical hits, critical hits, spell schools, healing, misses, and interrupts. Turn **Lock position** off to drag either list, then right-click a list to lock both. The minimap button is on and opens settings.

`/ct` opens settings. So does the addon compartment. `/ct test` shows sample lines.
