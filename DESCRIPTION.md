# Incoming Text

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Damage and healing that land on you, each in its own list.** Misses, and `+Combat` / `-Combat`, stay on the damage list. Each list has its own place and look.

**Damage** and **Healing** are separate settings pages. Each has enable, lock, font, size, crit size, opacity, fade time, lines, align, grow, and its own colors. The damage page also has **Show misses** and **Show combat**. **Show combat** prints `+Combat` on entering combat and `-Combat` on leaving. The main page has the same **Lock position**, font, size, opacity, fade time, lines, align, and grow rows. Changing one of those writes it onto both lists. Fonts from other addons appear when the shared media library is installed. Turn **Lock position** off to drag that list's area or either edge, then right-click it to lock. The minimap button is on by default and opens settings.

`/it` opens settings. So does the addon compartment. `/it test` shows sample lines.
