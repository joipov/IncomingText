# Incoming Text

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Damage, healing, and misses that land on you, in one list.** The list sits on the left. Damage uses the spell school color. A critical hit uses its own size and color.

**Show damage**, **Show healing**, and **Show misses** choose which lines appear.

Settings are **General** and **Colors**. **General** has **Font**, **Font size**, **Crit size**, **Opacity**, **Lines shown**, **Text align**, and **Grow**. Fonts from other addons appear when the shared media library is installed. **Colors** covers physical hits, critical hits, spell schools, healing, and misses. Turn **Lock position** off to drag the corner square, then right-click it to lock. The minimap button is on and opens settings.

`/it` opens settings. So does the addon compartment. `/it test` shows sample lines.
