# Incoming Text

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**Damage, healing, and status, each in its own list.** Misses stay on the damage list. `+Combat` and `-Combat` stay on the status list. Each list has its own place and look.

**Damage**, **Healing**, and **Status** are separate settings pages. Each has enable, lock, font, size, crit size, opacity, fade time, lines, align, grow, animate entry, and its own colors. **Text align** is left, center, or right. **Animate entry** fades a new line in and slides it into place. The damage page has **Show misses**. The status page has **Show combat**, which prints `+Combat` on entering combat and `-Combat` on leaving. The main page has the same **Lock position**, font, size, opacity, fade time, lines, align, grow, and animate entry rows. Changing one of those sets the same row on all three lists, and those rows move to the new value. Fonts from other addons appear when the shared media library is installed. Turn **Lock position** off to drag that list's area or an edge. Sample lines show where the text will sit until a real line is on screen. Right-click it to lock. The minimap button is on by default and opens settings.

`/it` opens settings. So does the addon compartment. `/it test` shows sample lines.
